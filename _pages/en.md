# Biography

He is pursuing a Ph.D. in Electronic Information at Peking University from 2024 to present, advised by [Associate Professor Yibo Lin](https://yibolin.com/) and co-advised by [Professor Guangyu Sun](https://gsunpku.github.io/). He received his undergraduate education from the Yao Class at Tsinghua University.

His research interests include system and technology co-optimization (STCO), with an emphasis on design space exploration (DSE), electronic design automation (EDA), and domain-specific accelerator architectures. His work focuses on holistic system performance modeling, AI-enhanced EDA workflows, and cross-layer optimization from chips to specialized hardware.
